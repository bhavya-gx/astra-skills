# Facts interview

Answer in your own words; short answers are fine. Save the result as `voice/<yourname>.md`. The reference-script skill writes only from this file, so what is not here is marked `[CHECK]` in the script instead of being invented.

1. **Who you are, in one line.** Role, company or practice, city. Written the way you say it on camera.
2. **Receipts you can say in public.** Numbers with their dates (followers, customers, revenue, years, awards). Only what you are allowed to publish, written exactly as you want it on screen ("62K", not "sixty-two thousand").
3. **The story in order.** Where it started, the turn, where it is now. Three to six dated moments. Real places and real names only where you have permission.
4. **Goals you have said out loud.** What you are building toward, in your words. Nothing aspirational that you have not actually said.
5. **People and companies you can name.** And the ones you cannot.
6. **Lines that are yours.** Phrases you say on purpose that the script may reuse.
7. **Things you never want said about you.** Framings, adjectives, comparisons.
8. **Spelling and style.** US or British, city names spelled out or not, how you write numbers, words you avoid.
9. **Footage you already have.** Clips and photos with dates, so B-roll lists can point at them.
10. **Three posts or videos of yours that worked.** Pasted or linked. The skill mirrors their rhythm, not their content.

Template for the saved file:

```markdown
# Facts: NAME
who: ...
receipts: 12 clients since March 2025; 3 years in business; ...
story: 2019 ... | 2022 ... | 2025 ...
goals: ...
can_name: ... ; cannot_name: ...
own_lines: "..." "..."
never: ...
style: US spelling; numbers as digits; "San Francisco" spelled out
footage: 2025-06 launch day clip; 2024-11 first office photo; ...
samples: (three, verbatim or linked)
```

`voice/example-facts.md` is a filled example for a fictional founder.
