# Founder Story

**Structure:** Problem → decision → useful change. Aim for 30–45 seconds after reading aloud; shorten to your natural pace.

## Fill-in spoken script

[PERSON] kept running into [SPECIFIC PROBLEM].

So they built [SOLUTION] for [AUDIENCE].

The interesting part is [DETAIL ABOUT THE APPROACH].

Instead of [OLD WAY], it lets people [NEW WAY].

Here is a concrete example: [SMALL REAL-WORLD SCENARIO].

The bigger lesson is [TAKEAWAY THAT GOES BEYOND THE PRODUCT].

[ONE QUESTION OR CALL TO ACTION]

## Ready-to-adapt example

The names and product in these examples are fictional; this is demonstration copy, not reporting. General AI behaviour should be checked against the particular product you discuss.

Maya kept seeing the same thing happen after team meetings: everyone agreed on the plan, but nobody knew where the next steps lived.

So she started building Loopdesk, a shared space for decisions and follow-ups.

The interesting part is what she left out. The idea was not another dashboard to maintain. It was one clear place to see what changed and who was doing what.

Imagine opening yesterday's meeting and finding three decisions, their owners, and the next action.

The bigger lesson? A useful product can start with one frustrating handoff.

What is the small problem your team keeps working around?

## On-screen beats

| Card | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| The problem | next steps disappeared after meetings |
| The decision | one shared place for decisions |
| The takeaway | start with a frustrating handoff |

Record the script first. Use the transcript to put these cards on the right phrases; a script alone does not establish subtitle timing. Keep each card short, and end with one clear action.
