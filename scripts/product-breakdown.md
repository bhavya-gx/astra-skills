# Product Breakdown

**Structure:** One promise → three specifics → fit. Aim for 30–45 seconds after reading aloud; shorten to your natural pace.

## Fill-in spoken script

If you [COMMON TASK], here is a tool worth understanding.

[PRODUCT] helps [AUDIENCE] do [CLEAR BENEFIT].

First, [FEATURE]. In practice, [EXAMPLE].

Second, [FEATURE]. That helps when [SITUATION].

Third, [FEATURE OR LIMITATION]. You still need to [HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY].

It is most useful for [PERSON / CONTEXT].

[ONE QUESTION OR CALL TO ACTION]

## Ready-to-adapt example

The names and product in these examples are fictional; this is demonstration copy, not reporting. General AI behaviour should be checked against the particular product you discuss.

If your team loses track of decisions after a meeting, here is a product idea worth understanding.

Loopdesk puts notes, decisions, and follow-ups in one shared place.

First, each decision gets an owner. In practice, “we should do this” becomes “Sam will send the first draft.”

Second, the next meeting starts with what changed. That helps when the conversation usually resets from scratch.

Third, the team reviews the summary before sharing it. A tidy note is only useful if it is accurate.

This would be most useful for small teams juggling several projects.

## On-screen beats

| Card | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Ownership | assign a person to each next step |
| 2. Continuity | start with what changed |
| 3. Review | check accuracy before sharing |

Record the script first. Use the transcript to put these cards on the right phrases; a script alone does not establish subtitle timing. Keep each card short, and end with one clear action.
